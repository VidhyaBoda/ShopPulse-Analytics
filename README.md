<p align="center">

**Turning customer transactions into actionable retail intelligence.**

</p>

---

## 📌 Project Overview

ShopPulse Analytics is an end-to-end customer shopping behavior analytics
project built using Python, PostgreSQL, SQL, and Power BI.

The project analyzes 3,900 retail purchase records to understand:

- Customer spending
- Product preferences
- Customer segmentation
- Discount behavior
- Subscription behavior
- Shipping preferences
- Review ratings
- Purchase frequency
- Age-group revenue contribution

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective is to transform customer transaction data into business
insights that can support:

- Customer engagement
- Revenue analysis
- Product positioning
- Marketing strategy
- Loyalty initiatives
- Subscription strategy
